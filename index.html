About Us

Our History

Muhanga Technical Center was established to provide quality technical and vocational education in Rwanda. Over the years, the school has grown into a center of excellence, producing skilled graduates in ICT, engineering, business, and hospitality sectors.

Our Mission

To equip students with practical skills, innovation, and discipline that prepare them for employment and entrepreneurship.

Our Vision

To become a leading technical training institution in Rwanda and East Africa.
